# Audits — Auditee

> Basics only for now — this article grows as the module does.

## What this module is for

Here the roles are reversed: an outside auditing body — an ISO registrar, the
FDA, a customer's quality team — audits **your** company. There's nothing for
you to walk through or score; your job is to be ready, host the audit, and
close out what the auditor finds. The Auditee module tracks exactly that. (If
you're the one running the audit, you want
[Audits — Auditor](./audits-auditor).)

## The flow, in short

1. **Create the audit** when the auditing body schedules one — date, site,
   your **Lead POC** and involved people, and the auditing body's contact
   (firm, auditor, email, phone). Referencing a standard from your library is
   optional — the auditor brings their own.
2. **Information tab** — scope, objectives, and the **Agenda**: upload the
   one the auditing body sent and keep your own preparation notes beside it.
3. **Reports tab** — after the visit the auditor issues a report. Upload it,
   write a short **Summary**, and record what it raised: **Findings** and
   **OFIs** (add them by hand, or let AI extract proposals from the PDF —
   you approve each one). Every finding remembers which report raised it.
4. **Close the loop** — findings link to CAPAs; when the CAPA closes, the
   finding can close. Interim reports may come mid-cycle; the **final
   report** typically follows once findings are addressed.
5. **Certificate tab** — when the audit passes, upload the certificate for
   future reference. **Mark Completed** closes the audit (it refuses while
   findings are still open).

## Before the auditor arrives

Use **Audit Readiness** (in the Audits menu) to see the gaps an auditor would
find first — stale open records, overdue document reviews, pending training,
calibration past due — and nudge the people responsible.
