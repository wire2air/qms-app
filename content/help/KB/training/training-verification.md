# Training Verification

## Overview

Completing training and being competent are not the same thing, and for the
training that matters your standard asks you to show the difference. Training
Verification is the step where a manager confirms the person can actually do the
task — after they have read the document and passed the quiz.

It is switched on per training, so it applies where competency genuinely needs
confirming and stays out of the way where read-and-understood is enough.

## How it works

1. A training instance is assigned and the person completes it.
2. If the training requires verification, it moves to **awaiting verification**
   rather than straight to complete.
3. It appears in the verifier's queue on the **Training Verification** page.
4. The verifier confirms competency — for one person or for several at once.
5. The instance completes and the record shows who verified it and when.

:::note A failed quiz still reaches the verifier
When someone exhausts their quiz retries, the instance moves to awaiting
verification rather than dying as a failure.

That is deliberate: failing a quiz is information for the manager, not a verdict.
They may retrain, verify competency another way, or record that the person is not
yet qualified — all of which are better outcomes than a dead-end status nobody
can act on.
:::

## Working the queue

The Training Verification page shows pending instances on the left and the
selected one on the right, with everyone still awaiting verification on it. It
also shows how many were closed this week, so a backlog is visible.

Verify people together where you have genuinely assessed them together — a group
you watched perform the task — and individually where you have not.

:::tip
Verification is a competency assertion with your name on it. Verify what you have
actually observed; "they completed the training" is what the completion record
already says, and repeating it here adds nothing an auditor will value.
:::

## Setting it up

Verification is enabled on the training itself, along with the quiz settings and
who the verifying manager is. See [Training](./training.md).

For document-driven training, the setting lives on the document's Training
Assessment tab — so releasing a new effective version retrains people and sends
them back through verification where it is required. See
[Document Control](../documents/document-control.md).
