# Troubleshooting

Use this page to diagnose common problems. If you're stuck on a specific feature,
open that feature's help article first.

## Quick diagnosis

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to try |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| A record won't save | Missing required field, or your session expired | Check fields marked required; sign out and back in |
| A field is read-only | The record left its editable status, or you lack permission | Confirm the status and your role |
| A button is greyed out | The action is restricted to a role/workflow step | Check whether the step is assigned to your role |
| A module is missing from the sidebar | Your role doesn't grant access | Ask an admin to review your permissions |
| Changes aren't showing | Stale view | Refresh the page |
| Didn't receive a notification | Not assigned, or event didn't fire | Check the Notifications center and your assignment |

## General steps

1. **Refresh** the page to rule out a stale view.
2. **Check required fields** — they're marked, and the form highlights what's
   missing on save.
3. **Confirm your role** has permission for the action.
4. **Note the exact error text** before retrying.

## Saving problems

If a record won't save:

- Make sure every required field is filled in.
- If you've been idle a long time, your session may have expired — sign out and
  back in, then re-enter your changes.
- Check that linked items (owner, site, related records) still exist.

## Approvals and workflow steps

If you can't complete a review or approval:

- Confirm the step is assigned to **your** role.
- Make sure any earlier steps are complete.
- Check whether the record is waiting on required evidence or fields.

:::tip
When contacting support, include what you were doing, the record's ID, and the
exact error message. That speeds up resolution.
:::
