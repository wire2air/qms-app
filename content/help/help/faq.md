# Frequently Asked Questions

## Access & accounts

### How do I get an account?
An administrator at your organization invites you. You'll receive an email with a
link to accept the invitation and set a password.

### Why can't I see a module or button?
Your access is controlled by the **roles** assigned to you. If something is missing,
ask your administrator to review your role and permissions.

### How is my data separated from other companies?
Each company has its own isolated workspace. You only ever see your organization's
records.

## Working with records

### What is a "record"?
Most things you create — a nonconformance, a CAPA, a document, a log entry — is a
record with a status, an owner, and a complete history.

### Why can't I edit a field?
Many records lock certain fields once they leave **Draft**, to protect the audit
trail. Editing is usually only allowed in an early/editable status, or by users with
the right permission.

### What do statuses mean?
Each record type moves through its own set of statuses (for example Draft → In
Review → Approved → Closed). The feature's help article lists its statuses.

## Tasks & approvals

### Where do my approvals and to-dos show up?
In **My Tasks**. Workflow steps assigned to you (reviews, approvals) and other
assignments appear there.

### Who can complete an approval step?
Only users with the role assigned to that workflow step. If a button is greyed out,
check whether the step is assigned to your role.

## Search & notifications

### How do I search the help center?
Use the search box in the navbar or press `Cmd/Ctrl + K`.

### Why didn't I get a notification?
Check the **Notifications** center in the app. Notifications are generated for
specific events; if you expected one, confirm you're assigned to the record or step.
