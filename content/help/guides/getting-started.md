# Getting Started

This guide walks you through your first 10 minutes in the Qability QMS.

## 1. Sign in

Open your workspace URL and sign in with the credentials from your invitation
email. If you were invited but haven't set a password yet, use the link in the
invitation to accept it and choose a password.

:::tip
Forgot your password? Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in screen.
:::

## 2. Get oriented

The **left sidebar** is your map. It's split into your day-to-day **work area** at
the top and **Settings** (administration) at the bottom.

Common starting points:

- **Dashboard** — a summary of activity and what needs your attention
- **My Tasks** — everything assigned to you (reviews, approvals, training, forms)
- **Documents**, **Nonconformances**, **CAPAs** — the core quality records

## 3. Find what's assigned to you

Open **My Tasks** to see your inbox of work. Tasks are grouped by kind (for example
approvals, training, and form submissions) and show their priority and due date.
Click any task to open the record and act on it.

## 4. Use search

Press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` (or click the search box in the top bar) to jump straight to
any help article.

## 5. Learn the building blocks

A few concepts apply across the whole product:

- Most things you create are **records** with a **status**, an **owner**, and a
  full **history**.
- **Roles** decide what you can see and do.
- **Sites** and **departments** scope records to the right part of your
  organization.

Read the **QMS Overview** for the big picture.

## Next steps

Pick the area you'll use most from the sidebar — for example **Nonconformances**,
**CAPAs**, **Document Control**, **Inspections & Logs**, or **Training** — and
follow the "How to…" sections in that article.
